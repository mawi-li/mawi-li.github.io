Scope

This page describes the Mawi.li website. Individual apps, games and external services may have separate privacy information.

02

Your preferences

The website stores your selected language and appearance in local storage in your browser. You can remove these preferences by clearing this website’s data in your browser settings.

03

Browsing and hosting

Site search runs in your browser. The site does not add analytics or advertising trackers. The hosting provider processes network requests to deliver the pages; its own privacy terms also apply.

04

Contacting the studio

Email links open your email application. When you choose to send an email, its contents and sender information are handled by your email provider and the studio’s mailbox to respond to your enquiry.

05

External links

When you follow an external project or download link, that service has its own data practices. Review its privacy information as needed.

06

Questions

For questions about information shared with the studio, use the email address on the contact page.
