Ideas become experiences.

Mawi.li is an independent studio for software, websites and games. It is a home for different projects, connected by a shared interest in building useful and enjoyable digital experiences.

Clarity comes first.

A project should explain what it offers, which platforms it supports and whether it is ready to use. We distinguish work in development from available releases.
